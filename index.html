🌱 Low-Cost Prevention

  • Inspect leaves regularly for changes.
  • Water near the soil instead of soaking foliage.
  • Allow enough space for airflow between plants.
  • Remove affected plant material when appropriate.
  • Use clean tools and healthy planting material.
  • Confirm the cause before applying crop treatments.
